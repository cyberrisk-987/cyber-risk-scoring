Financial impact

Scoring guidance
  • Low: negligible exposure, easily absorbed.
  • Medium: small costs or minimal unrealised loss.
  • High: noticeable response, recovery or revenue costs.
  • Very high: major costs, potential fines or customer loss.
  • Critical: severe, prolonged and material business impact.

Reputational impact

Scoring guidance
  • Low: no external visibility or customer impact.
  • Medium: limited visibility and minimal concern.
  • High: noticeable concern, complaints or limited data exposure.
  • Very high: widespread attention and clear erosion of trust.
  • Critical: sustained scrutiny and severe lasting brand harm.

03

Threat assessment

Assess the most likely threat actor's motivation and capability.

Motivation

Scoring guidance
  • Low: little value or CIA impact.
  • Medium: limited information or minor disruption.
  • High: useful data, financial gain or operational disruption.
  • Very high: sensitive data or significant service disruption.
  • Critical: major gain or severe CIA impact.

Capability

Scoring guidance
  • Low: basic scanners and common misconfigurations.
  • Medium: public exploit kits and simple web flaws.
  • High: organised groups using known vulnerabilities.
  • Very high: skilled groups or insiders with custom tooling.
  • Critical: highly advanced actors and zero-days.

04

Vulnerability assessment

Assess ease of exploitation and exposure to the threat.

Exposure to threat

Scoring guidance
  • Low: highly restricted with strong controls.
  • Medium: limited exposure with multiple controls.
  • High: reachable in certain environments.
  • Very high: externally or broadly accessible.
  • Critical: fully exposed with minimal controls.

Ease of exploitation

Scoring guidance
  • Low: rare zero-day and advanced expertise required.
  • Medium: specialist knowledge or complex conditions.
  • High: moderate effort using known techniques.
  • Very high: public exploit code or tools available.
  • Critical: trivial, unauthenticated or widely exploited.

Assessment summary

The component calculations use five bands, then the resulting levels are plotted on the final 5 × 5 risk matrix.

Scoring boundaries

Component scores: Low 1–4, Medium 5–8, High 9–12, Very high 13–19, Critical 20–25. Each raw score maps to a level from 1 to 5.

Final matrix: Low 1–4, Medium 5–12, Very high 13–25. The exact matrix score remains visible, so a score of 5 can be distinguished from a score of 12 even though both are Medium.
